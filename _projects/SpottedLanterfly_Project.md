This project was developed as part of our MAE 2250 Open Design Project and focuses on designing, prototyping, and testing a light-based mechanical trapping system to reduce spotted lanternfly (SLF) interaction with grapevines. Our team identified SLF contact with vines as a key contributor to yield and quality loss and engineered a preventative solution that attracts SLFs using light and mechanically forces them into containment before they can damage crops.
